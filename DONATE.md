# Support Just Kanban

Just Kanban is developed independently and distributed free of charge.
There is no paid tier, no ads, no tracking, and no telemetry. If the
app has been useful to you, you can support its development with a
donation.

## Why donate?

- The project has no monetization and no Apple Developer account
- Donations help cover development time and future infrastructure
  (signing, hosting, potential Apple Developer enrollment)
- Donations do not grant access to features, support, or roadmap
  priority

## ⚠️ Critical: Network Selection

Sending the wrong token on the wrong network will result in **permanent
loss of funds**. Double-check the network in your wallet before sending.

- **USDT** must be sent on the **Tron (TRC-20)** network. Do NOT send
  USDT via ERC-20 (Ethereum) — the address is not configured for it.
- **USDC** must be sent on the **Base** network. Do NOT send USDC via
  Ethereum mainnet, Polygon, or Arbitrum.
- The **ETH and USDC (Base) addresses are identical** because Base is
  EVM-compatible. Same private key, same address — but the token and
  network must match.

- This is not an investment, pre-order, or purchase of any product
- Donations do not entitle the sender to compensation, equity, or

## Verifying Official Addresses

These addresses are published in this public GitHub repository and in
the official README. If you find these addresses posted anywhere else
(third-party sites, social media, ads), treat them with suspicion —
address-poisoning scams are common.

Always cross-reference with the official repository before sending.
